A library is a quiet and peaceful treasure trove of knowledge filled with books, reference materials, storybooks, and digital resources. It plays a vital role in student life and community learning by encouraging the habit of reading, improving academic performance, and supporting independent research. Inside a library, students and visitors can sit down to study, borrow books for home, or explore different subjects like science, history, and literature away from distractions. Maintained by a helpful librarian, the library creates a calm environment that fosters curiosity, critical thinking, and a lifelong love for learning
